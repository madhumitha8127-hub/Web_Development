How we got here

Started in a home kitchen with one secondhand oven

In 2016, Karuppasamy Preetham was baking forty loaves a week out of his flat and delivering them on a scooter. The queue outside his building got long enough that the neighbours complained, which is roughly how Sweet Crumbs found a shopfront.

Ten years on, not much has changed about the method. Long ferments, real butter, no premixes, nothing frozen. What changed is that there are now six of us, and the oven is ours.
